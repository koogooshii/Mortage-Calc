Realtor Checklist & Buyer's Guide

Essential steps and checks your realtor should perform when buying a home.

Critical Due Diligence

  • MPAC & City Records: Verify legal description, lot size, and zoning matches municipal records.
  • Permit History: Check for open permits and ensure past renovations (decks, basements) were permitted and closed.
  • Title Search: Confirm clear title, ownership, and check for liens or easements.

Financial & Taxes

  • Land Transfer Tax: Calculate provincial and municipal taxes and identify rebates.
  • Market Analysis: Provide a Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) to ensure fair pricing.

Property Condition

  • Inspections: Arrange home inspection and specialized checks (radon, mold, pests).
  • Renovation Check: Cross-reference permits with physical condition to identify unpermitted work.

Closing & Future

  • Future Value: Advise on future development plans or zoning changes in the area.
  • Closing: Coordinate conditions, final walk-through, and key handover.
